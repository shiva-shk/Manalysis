SSupplements Ledger 1

Iran's dietary supplement market by ingredient family, product line and company, FY1390–1404 (Persian calendar. FY1404 runs roughly Mar 2025 to Mar 2026).

Figures are summed from the supplement producers syndicate yearbooks (sales by manufacturers to distributors), cleaned and combined into one ledger. FY1390 to FY1395 come from a single combined yearbook, FY1396 to FY1404 from yearly editions. A product line is one brand (or product name) of one company in one dosage form, matched across years by name, so a renamed product shows up as a new line. Ingredient families and categories are keyword groupings built from what each company reported. Values are nominal rials and are not adjusted for inflation. The FY1403 edition lists far fewer companies than FY1402 and its publication date looks mistyped, so year on year changes into and out of FY1403 should be read with care.
